# Channel Guidance Reference

For each of the 2-4 prioritized channels chosen in Workflow step 5, include the content below. Only elaborate on channels actually prioritized — deferred channels get one line stating the deferral condition, not a full plan. Every claim here is a **Recommendation** or general practice, not a fact about the user's specific business — ground it in their actual data where supplied.

## Email & SMS

- Key automated flows to set up: welcome series, abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back.
- Campaign types: new-product announcements, sales/promotions, content newsletters.
- List-building tactics: pop-up offer, checkout opt-in, social media lead magnets.
- Reference target metrics (see `marketing-benchmarks.md` for sourcing): open rate >35%, click rate >3%, revenue per email >$0.10 — present these as benchmarks to compare against, not guarantees.

## SEO & Content

- Keyword themes (directional only — full keyword research and technical SEO diagnosis are out of this skill's scope; for page-level SEO issues, point to `ecommerce-seo-auditor`).
- Content types to consider: blog posts, buying guides, comparison pages, FAQ content.
- Publishing cadence: state a specific number of posts per week/month appropriate to the business's stage and capacity — never a generic "post often."
- Note any technical SEO priorities only at a high level; defer specifics to a dedicated audit.

## Paid Ads

- Recommend platforms and why, based on the audience and product (e.g. visual products → Meta/TikTok; high-intent search → Google).
- Suggest a budget split across recommended platforms, from `marketing-benchmarks.md`'s allocation tables adjusted to the business's actual budget.
- Distinguish campaign types: awareness vs. conversion.
- This skill produces a high-level ads brief only — detailed campaign setup, ad copy, and creative are out of scope; note that as a next step requiring a dedicated execution workflow if the user has one.

## Social Media

- Platform selection based on where the target audience actually spends time (see persona's "where they discover products").
- Content pillars: 3-4 themes to rotate, grounded in the product/brand, not generic filler.
- Posting frequency appropriate to the team's actual capacity (see Workflow step 7's owners/capacity check).
- Brief engagement strategy: how to respond to comments/DMs, what counts as meaningful engagement for this stage.

## Influencer & Affiliate

- Type of influencer to target: micro vs. macro, niche-specific vs. broad — based on budget and stage.
- Outreach approach and what to offer (product seeding, flat fee, commission, or a mix).
- Commission/payment structure options, if affiliate is prioritized.
- How to measure ROI for this channel specifically (unique codes, referral links, attributed revenue).

## Referral & Loyalty

- Program structure options: points-based, tiered, or straight referral rewards.
- When to launch: only once there's a sufficient existing customer base to seed it — state the rough threshold for this business's stage.
- Expected impact, framed as a **Recommendation** referencing the retention stats in `marketing-benchmarks.md`, not a guaranteed lift.
