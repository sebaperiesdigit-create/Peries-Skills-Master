## Budget allocation framework (overall)

| Channel | % of budget | Priority |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Paid ads (Google, Meta, TikTok) | 25-35% | P1 — immediate traffic |
| Content + SEO | 20-25% | P2 — long-term compound growth |
| Email + SMS | 15-20% | P1 — highest ROI, retention |
| Social media (organic) | 10-15% | P3 — brand building |
| Influencer / affiliate | 10-15% | P2 — credibility + reach |
| Testing / new channels | 5-10% | P3 — discover the next winner |

Recommended overall split (Omnisend 2026 Ecommerce Report): **60% owned/organic (SEO, email, content) + 40% paid channels (PPC, social ads, influencers).**

Cross-checked against: Azarian Growth Agency 2025 (25-30% content/SEO, 20-25% paid search, 15-20% paid social, 10-15% email); Gartner 2025 CMO Survey (marketing budgets averaging 7.7% of company revenue). The stage-specific splits below are synthesized from these sources — treat them as a starting point, adjust based on the user's actual data and goals, and never present the synthesis itself as a cited figure.

## Customer retention reference stats

- Repeat customers make up 21% of the customer base but drive 44% of revenue (Omnisend).
- Purchase-again probability: after the 1st purchase, ~27% buy again → after the 2nd, ~49% → after the 3rd, ~62% (Smile.io).
- Businesses using 3+ marketing channels see a 287% higher purchase rate than single-channel businesses (Omnisend).

## KPI benchmark reference

| Metric | Reported benchmark | How to set a target | How to track |
|---|:---:|---|---|
| CAC | Varies by category | Must stay below profit per order (AOV × margin) | Ad platforms + analytics |
| LTV | Varies by category | Aim for > 3× CAC; below that, acquisition is too expensive | Platform analytics |
| Email list growth | 5-10%/month | New stores: ~5%/mo target. Growing: 8-10%/mo | Email platform |
| Email revenue share | 25-30% of total | Start at a 15% target, grow toward 25%+ over 6 months | Email platform |
| Organic traffic growth | 10-20%/month | SEO typically takes 3-6 months to show results; ~10%/mo after month 3 is a reasonable target | Analytics platform |
| Social engagement rate | 1-3% | Below 1% usually signals a content problem; 2%+ is a reasonable target | Platform analytics |
| Repeat purchase rate | ~28% average | New stores: 15-20%. Growing: 25%+. Established: 30%+ | Platform analytics |
| Blended ROAS | 2.5x+ | Calculate a minimum: 1 ÷ margin × 1.5 | All ad platforms combined |
